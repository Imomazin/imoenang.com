CURRENT WORK

Scholarship connected to practice.

Imo is a Senior Lecturer and Programme Leader in Project Management with Greenwich Online at the University of Greenwich. His academic work spans organisational ambidexterity, strategic management, digital strategy, AI-enabled decision governance, project management, innovation and sustainability.

Alongside academia, he leads VivaLaVida (VLV), connecting technology, practical capability and business growth through Ambidexters, SkillHubs and ImpactCity.
