SCHOLAR AND PRACTITIONER

Research grounded in organisational practice.

Dr Enang is Programme Head for Project Management with Greenwich Online at the University of Greenwich. His academic work covers organisational ambidexterity, strategy, project management, digital transformation, decision governance, innovation and sustainability.

His professional career spans higher education, Procter & Gamble, banking, consulting and entrepreneurship across Europe, the Middle East and Africa. He leads VLV Group, connecting Ambidexters, ImpactCity and SkillHubs across technology, visibility and intelligence, training and consultancy.

His work is built around one consistent question: how can rigorous strategy be translated into decisions, systems and capabilities that organisations can use?
