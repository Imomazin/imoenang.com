EXECUTIVE PRACTICE

Strategy tested in real organisations.

Dr Imo Enang's practice spans commercial leadership, transformation, project management, consulting, executive education and entrepreneurship. As CEO of VLV Group, he leads a connected operating model across technology, visibility and intelligence, training and consultancy.

INDUSTRY FOUNDATION

Commercial and operational leadership.

Dr Enang spent more than a decade with Procter & Gamble, working across commercial operations, strategy, marketing and organisational execution in multinational markets.

His later work has included banking and digital transformation, management consulting, project and programme delivery, knowledge exchange, executive education and business advisory work.

This background gives his scholarship a practical reference point. Strategy is treated as a system of choices, capabilities, governance and execution with choices carried through into practice.
