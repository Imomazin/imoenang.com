RESEARCH THEMES

A coherent strategy research agenda.

Organisational ambidexterity remains a central theme, with emphasis on implementation, dynamic capabilities, organisational culture, design, market orientation and performance.

A second strand examines how organisations convert information into accountable strategic decisions. This includes decision architecture, digital strategy, trust, human accountability and the design of management systems under uncertainty.

A third strand focuses on project and programme management, especially capability, resistance, transformation, governance and adaptive delivery.
