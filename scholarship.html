SCHOLARSHIP

Strategy, ambidexterity and organisational decision-making.

Dr Imo Enang publishes across strategic management, organisational ambidexterity, project management, digital transformation, leadership and decision governance. His research examines how organisations make decisions, manage change and implement strategy.
