THE GROUP MODEL

Technology, capability and visibility designed to reinforce one another.

VLV Group operates through three specialist studios. ImpactCity addresses visibility, authority, organisational intelligence and research. SkillHubs builds capability through training and consultancy. Ambidexters builds the software and digital systems that make solutions repeatable and scalable.

The studios share people, templates, pricing logic and one client record. Each engagement is routed to the studio that owns the work, but the wider group can combine disciplines when a business problem crosses technology, capability and market visibility.

Dr Imo Enang leads VLV Group as CEO and founder. The group forms the principal entrepreneurial platform connecting his work in strategy, transformation, executive education, technology and research-led practice.

RESEARCH AND KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE

OPERATING STANDARD

One brief, one client record and measured outcomes.

01

Brief: outcome, decision owner and scope agreed in writing.

02

Discovery: current position, audience and baseline audited.

03

Build: assets, content, platform or programme built to scope.

04

Activate: launch, delivery and handover tracked in the client record.

05

Measure: results checked against the original brief on an agreed date, then iterated or scaled.
