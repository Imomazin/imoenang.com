ABOUT DR IMO ENANG

Scholarship connected to practice.

Dr Imo Enang is a scholar-practitioner, academic leader, author, global consultant and CEO of VLV Group. His work connects strategy, organisational ambidexterity, project management, digital transformation and executive decision-making.

CURRENT WORK

Academic leadership and executive practice.

Dr Enang is Programme Head for Project Management with Greenwich Online at the University of Greenwich. His responsibilities span curriculum development, academic quality, assessment, student experience, employability, industry engagement and scholarly activity.

He is also CEO of VLV Group, the connected group bringing together Ambidexters, ImpactCity and SkillHubs. The group combines technology and venture building, visibility and intelligence, training and consultancy under one operating model.

Across both roles he works at the boundary between evidence and execution. His focus is the design of practical systems that help leaders make better strategic decisions and build organisations that can adapt without losing coherence.
