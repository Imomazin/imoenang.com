ABOUT DR IMO ENANG

Research, teaching and executive practice.

Dr Imo Enang is a multi-award-winning scholar-practitioner, global strategist, published author, international consultant, academic leader and entrepreneur. As Founder & CEO of VLV Group, he connects scholarship with executive practice and venture building.

CURRENT WORK

Academic leadership and executive practice.

Dr Enang is Programme Head for Project Management with Greenwich Online at the University of Greenwich. His responsibilities span curriculum development, academic quality, assessment, student experience, employability, industry engagement and scholarly activity.

He is also Founder & CEO of VLV Group, the connected group bringing together Ambidexters, ImpactCity and SkillHubs. The group combines technology and venture building, visibility and intelligence, training and consultancy under one operating model.

His work focuses on strategic decision-making, organisational design and how organisations adapt to change.
